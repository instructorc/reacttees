If you’re a parent with multiple young children, these 7 tips will change your life!!

Whether you are a parent or someone who cares for multiple young children, these tips will definitely help you manage the chaos.

1) Stop folding clothes and start sorting

If you are still trying to fold the numerous loads of laundry, please do us all a favor and stop. In this season of life, the best thing to do with kids clothes is to sort them into color coded buckets by clothes item. For example, put long pants in the green bucket and socks in the yellow bucket.

The color-coded buckets help kids learn their colors and memorize where to find thier clothes. For instance, Johnny will always know that his socks are in the yellow bucket.

2) Start Carrying the baby the right way...if you are like me you have been carrying your kids the wrong way!!

Yup, this tip pretty much changed my life...without further ado, please view the video below.

3) Paper Goods....use them!!

I know...I know paper goods are a waste of money. If you are worried about the environment, you can purchase compostable paper goods. Nobody likes clean-up, but taking a rag and pulling up the garbage can to the table makes cleaning up a breeze.

4) Invest in a powerful sucker(AKA..vacuum cleaner)

Sometimes it may feel like none of the food went into their mouths. Does your floor look like the one in the picture below...if so, you're doing good. I've seen worse. Put the broom away...until all your kids are over the age of 4, please save yourself some time and vacuum the messes that seem to happen every 5 minutes. You may want to pick up the wet stuff, or you can get a wet/dry vac that will handle every situation. Just make sure to dump and clean the vac after every use.

5) Do not give candy away freely...Bribe them!

Bribery is not evil, it is a very powerful control tool for little ones under the age of 5. Steward it wisely. At times, you will feel like sweets have more power than the parents. Most of us have experienced the meme below.

6) Double the size of your bed immediately

No matter how hard you try to keep them out of your bed they always find a way in. Some may go as far as thinking your bed is theirs. So.....if you have a full-size bed, purchase a king-size bed. If you already have a king-size bed, plan to purchase another in the next 4 years because the one you currently have will be absolutely ruined. They will jump, spill, and pee all over it.

7) If you have the money and space, purchase these three items!!!

A trampoline, A kiddie pool and a tablet device (older models preferably). You can find all of these items on Ebay or on Craigslist. Click on the highlighted links to see how cheap you can get some of these items.
