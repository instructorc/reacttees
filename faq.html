Frequently Asked Questions

When can I expect my tee to arrive?

You can typically expect your tee to arrive within 3 - 7 business days. The average delivery time is typically 4 days.

What should I do if I'm not pleased with my order

Please email us at service@reacttees.com and someone will touch basis with you within the next 2 hours to help resolve the matter.

Who do I contact if my order has not arrived?

Please email us at service@reacttees.com and someone will reply with 1-hour. Reasons for tee not arriving can be subject to wrong address, theft, a delay in delivery, etc. We will do our best to track down your order.


  • 1. Wrong address - we will confirm we have the right address
  • 2. Theft - we check with the shipping company to see if they successfully delivered package
  • 3. Delay in delivery - the package may still be in route or delivery company is experience longer than average delivery times

Do you accept bulk orders?

Yes, Yes, and Yes! If the quantity is greater than 5 shirts, please contact us for special pricing! You can email us at service@reacttees.com and someone will reply within 1 hour of of your request.

Do you offer customization of one of your tee's?

Typically, we do not customize our design's, but send all inquires to service@reacttees.com if you would like us to provide you with a qoute on any adjustments to our original Tee's.

Some adjustments may include:


  • 1. Changing and adding text
  • 2. Providing a specific color
  • 3. Using a specific type of shirt, or material
